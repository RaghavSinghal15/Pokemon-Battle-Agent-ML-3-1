**Homework for everyone in week 1:** play 10 to 15 Random Battles on
<https://play.pokemonshowdown.com> (choose the format "[Gen 9] Random Battle"; no account or
download needed). The examiner will ask why the problem is hard, and that answer sounds very
different from someone who has actually played.
